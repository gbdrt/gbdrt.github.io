Guillaume Baudart

Researcher at Inria

guillaume [dot] baudart [at] inria.fr

I am a researcher at Inria in the PICUBE team at the IRIF laboratory. My research focuses on generative AI for interactive theorem proving (agent-prover interfaces, tools, specialized models), and on probabilistic programming languages (language design, semantics, static analysis, compilation, inference).

Since 2024, I have co-led the Inria Défi LLM4Code, and I started LLM4Rocq research group. From 2020 to 2023 I was a member of the PARKAS team at the Département d'Informatique (DI) of the École normale supérieure (ENS). From 2017 to 2020 I was a research staff member at the IBM T.J. Watson Research Center in the AI Programming Models team.
